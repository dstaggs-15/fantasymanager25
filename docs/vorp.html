Interactive VORP Calculator

How to Use This Tool

VORP (Value Over Replacement Player) measures a player's true value by comparing their fantasy output to a "replacement-level" player—the kind of player you could find on the waiver wire.

  1. Set Replacement Levels: In the boxes below, enter the rank for the replacement player at each position. For a 10-team league, defaults like QB11, RB22, WR32, and TE11 are a good start.
  2. Analyze the Rankings: The table will instantly update. A higher VORP score means a player gives you a bigger weekly advantage over your opponents.
  3. Experiment: Change the numbers to see how player values shift based on position scarcity. For example, increasing the TE number to TE15 will make elite TEs even more valuable.
