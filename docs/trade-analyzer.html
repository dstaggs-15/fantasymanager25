How The Trade Analyzer Works

This tool evaluates a trade from two perspectives to create a final Trade Grade.

1. Season-Long Value (70% of Grade)

This is calculated using VORP (Value Over Replacement Player). VORP measures a player's total value over the entire season compared to a waiver-wire level player. A high total VORP indicates a stronger roster for the long haul.

2. Immediate Impact (30% of Grade)

This is calculated using the weekly Start Score. This score tells you who is getting the more immediately startable assets for the upcoming week's matchup.

The final grade combines these factors to provide a holistic view of the trade, balancing long-term and short-term impact.
