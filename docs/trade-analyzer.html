Trade Analyzer

How The Trade Grade is Calculated

The final Trade Grade is a comprehensive evaluation based on a 7-Factor Model. Each factor is scored on a 0-10 scale and then combined in a weighted average to produce a final score from 0-100, which is then converted to a letter grade.

Core Value Factors

These factors make up the bulk of the grade and measure a player's raw fantasy value.

  • ROS Projection (30% Weight): The most important factor. This is our "homegrown" Rest-of-Season projection, calculated from a weighted average of a player's recent performance. It measures a player's expected future value.
  • Proven Production (20% Weight): This grounds the projections in reality by looking at a player's season-long Points Per Game (PPG).
  • Player Tier (15% Weight): This grades a player based on their ROS projection rank, sorting them into tiers (e.g., Elite, Great, Good). Acquiring a higher-tier player provides more value.

Contextual & Roster-Fit Factors

These factors add crucial context beyond a simple point projection.

  • Weekly Upside (10% Weight): Measures immediate impact using the weekly Start Score.
  • Consistency (10% Weight): Uses a player's Standard Deviation to measure volatility. Acquiring a more consistent (lower Std Dev) player improves this score.
  • Efficiency (10% Weight): An advanced metric that measures a player's effectiveness with their opportunities (e.g., Yards Per Touch, TD Rate).
  • Team Offense (5% Weight): Grades the quality of a player's offensive environment. Players on high-scoring teams have more opportunities to score.
