How The 8-Factor Trade Grade is Calculated

The final grade is determined by calculating a Trade Value (0-100) for each player based on a weighted average of eight distinct factors. The total value of all players on each side of the trade is compared to determine the final letter grade.

Core Value Factors (60%)

  • ROS Projections (30%): Our homegrown Rest-of-Season projection, measuring a player's expected future value.
  • Proven Production (15%): A player's historical Points Per Game (PPG), measuring their reliable floor.
  • Player Tier (15%): A grade based on a player's rank within their position (Elite, Great, etc.).

Contextual & Roster-Fit Factors (40%)

  • Weekly Upside (10%): Uses the "Start Score" for the upcoming week to measure immediate impact.
  • Roster Consistency (10%): Measures volatility using Standard Deviation.
  • Player Efficiency (5%): An advanced metric using VORP (Value Over Replacement Player).
  • Player Age (5%): Rewards younger players with more long-term value.
  • Team Offense (5%): Grades the quality of the player's offensive environment.
