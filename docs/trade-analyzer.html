How The 8-Factor Trade Grade is Calculated

The final grade is determined by calculating a Trade Value (0-100) for each player based on a weighted average of eight distinct factors. The total value of all players on each side of the trade is compared to determine the final letter grade.

Core Value Factors (60%)

  • ROS Projections (30%): Our homegrown Rest-of-Season projection. This is the most heavily weighted factor as it measures a player's expected future value for the remainder of the season.
  • Proven Production (15%): A player's historical Points Per Game (PPG). This grounds the projection in historical reality and measures a player's reliable floor.
  • Player Tier (15%): A grade based on a player's rank within their position, sorting them into tiers (Elite, Great, etc.). Acquiring higher-tier players provides more value.

Contextual & Roster-Fit Factors (40%)

  • Weekly Upside (10%): Uses the 4-Factor "Start Score" for the upcoming week to measure a player's immediate impact and short-term value.
  • Roster Consistency (10%): A measure of volatility using Standard Deviation. A lower Std Dev (more consistent player) results in a higher score.
  • Player Efficiency (5%): An advanced metric using VORP (Value Over Replacement Player) as a proxy for overall efficiency and impact.
  • Player Age (5%): A score that rewards younger players, who have more long-term and dynasty value.
  • Team Offense (5%): A grade for the quality of the player's offensive environment. Players on high-scoring teams have more opportunities.
