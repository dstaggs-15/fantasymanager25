How is the "Start Score" Calculated?

The Start Score is a comprehensive rating from 1-10 that provides a data-driven recommendation for the upcoming week. It is a weighted average of four key factors:

1. Player Talent (40% Weight)

This measures a player's baseline skill. It's calculated using their season-long Points Per Game (PPG) and is normalized against the top player at their position. A higher PPG results in a higher talent score.

2. Weekly Matchup (30% Weight)

This grades the difficulty of the opponent. It's based on our defensive rankings, which calculate how many fantasy points each NFL team allows to each position. Facing the #1 easiest defense gives a high score, while facing the #32 hardest defense gives a low score.

3. Offensive Line (15% Weight)

This scores the quality of a player's blocking. It uses our "homegrown" O-line rankings, which are generated by analyzing a team's run blocking (based on EPA per rush) and pass blocking (based on sack rate).

4. Efficiency (15% Weight)

This measures how effective a player is with their opportunities and how well they protect the ball.

  • For QBs: A weighted score combining Touchdown Rate, Yards Per Attempt, Interception Rate (negative), and Fumble Rate (negative).
  • For RB/WR/TEs: A weighted score combining Yards Per Touch (positive) and Fumble Rate (negative).

These four scores are combined using the weights above to produce the final "Start Score."
