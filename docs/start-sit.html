How the "Start Score" is Calculated

The Start Score is a weighted average of four key factors to provide a comprehensive recommendation for the upcoming week. A higher score is better.

  • Talent (40%): A player's season-long Points Per Game (PPG).
  • Matchup (30%): The defensive ranking of their opponent against their position.
  • O-Line (15%): The rank of the player's offensive line.
  • Efficiency (15%): A player's Yards Per Touch (for RB/WR/TE) or TD Rate (for QB).
