How the "Start Score" is Calculated (In-Depth)

The Start Score is a comprehensive rating from 0-10 that provides a data-driven recommendation for the upcoming week. It is a weighted average of four key factors, each normalized to a 0-10 scale.

1. Player Talent (40% Weight)

This measures a player's baseline skill and typical production. It's calculated using their season-long Points Per Game (PPG). To turn PPG into a 0-10 score, we normalize it against the top-performing player at their same position. This means the #1 PPG player at a position gets a 10, and everyone else is scored relative to them.

Talent Score = (Player's PPG / Max PPG at Position) * 10

2. Weekly Matchup (30% Weight)

This grades the difficulty of the opponent. It's based on our defensive rankings, which calculate how many fantasy points each NFL defense allows to each position (1st = easiest, 32nd = hardest). A better matchup results in a higher score.

Matchup Score = ((32 - Opponent's Rank) / 31) * 10

3. Offensive Line (15% Weight)

This scores the quality of a player's blocking. It uses our "homegrown" O-line rankings, which are generated by analyzing a team's run blocking (EPA per rush) and pass blocking (sack rate). A better O-line rank results in a higher score.

O-Line Score = ((32 - Team's O-Line Rank) / 31) * 10

4. Efficiency (15% Weight)

This measures how effective a player is with their opportunities and how well they protect the ball. The calculation is specific to the player's position:

  • For QBs: A weighted score combining Touchdown Rate (positive), Yards Per Attempt (positive), Interception Rate (negative), and Fumble Rate (negative).
  • For RBs: A weighted score combining Yards Per Carry and Yards Per Reception.
  • For WRs & TEs: A score based purely on their Yards Per Reception.

Each component is normalized against an "elite" benchmark (e.g., 5.5 Yards Per Carry for an RB) to generate the final 0-10 score.

These four scores are then combined using their weights to produce the final Start Score you see on the player cards.
